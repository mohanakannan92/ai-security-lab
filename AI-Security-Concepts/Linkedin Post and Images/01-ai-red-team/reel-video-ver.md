🎬 **AI Security Reel Script (45–60 sec)**

**[Scene 1 — Hook | 0–3 sec]**
📹 Visual: Big bold text on screen
❌ “0% Data Leaks”
➡️ Cut to
🔐 “Secure AI System?”

🎙️ Voiceover:
“0% data leaks doesn’t mean your AI is secure.”

---

**[Scene 2 — Setup | 3–8 sec]**
📹 Visual: You coding / dashboard / terminal
Text overlay: “I built an AI system…”

🎙️ Voiceover:
“I built an AI system that looked completely safe.”

---

**[Scene 3 — False Confidence | 8–12 sec]**
📹 Visual: Green checkmarks animation
✅ No leaks
✅ Safe responses
✅ Filters working

🎙️ Voiceover:
“No leaks. Safe responses. Everything looked perfect.”

---

**[Scene 4 — Reality Hit | 12–18 sec]**
📹 Visual: Glitch effect / red warning
⚠️ “50% attacks bypassed”

🎙️ Voiceover:
“But when I tested real attacks… 50% bypassed my defenses.”

---

**[Scene 5 — Insight | 18–24 sec]**
📹 Visual: Clean text, slow zoom
💥 “Security ≠ Just Output”

🎙️ Voiceover:
“Security isn’t just about output… it’s about what you fail to block.”

---

**[Scene 6 — Solution | 24–35 sec]**
📹 Visual: Fast cuts (code, dashboard, charts)
Text overlays:
🔴 Attack Simulation
🟢 Defense Layers
📊 Evaluation + Dashboard

🎙️ Voiceover:
“So I built an AI Security Red Team Lab to test systems like attackers do.”

---

**[Scene 7 — Attacks | 35–42 sec]**
📹 Visual: List popping one by one
• Prompt Injection
• Role Override
• Data Extraction
• Multi-step attacks

🎙️ Voiceover:
“It simulates real attacks like prompt injection and data extraction.”

---

**[Scene 8 — Results | 42–50 sec]**
📹 Visual: Your dashboard screenshot
📈 “80% blocked | 20% bypass | 0% leaks”

🎙️ Voiceover:
“Now I can clearly see where my system is strong… and where it breaks.”

---

**[Scene 9 — Takeaway | 50–56 sec]**
📹 Visual: Bold text
🧠 “Looks safe ≠ Is safe”

🎙️ Voiceover:
“A system can look safe… and still be easy to manipulate.”

---

**[Scene 10 — CTA | 56–60 sec]**
📹 Visual: You / clean background text
💬 “How are you testing AI security?”

🎙️ Voiceover:
“How are you testing AI security in your projects?”
